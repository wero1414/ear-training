What trains what

Absolute pitch barely transfers to playing. The skills that do are functional: hearing a note as a scale degree inside a key, hearing a chord's quality, and hearing where a progression is going. Chapters C and D are the ones that show up when you improvise.

  • Degrees before intervals. Once 4 sounds like 4 rather than "a fourth above something", you can find a melody by ear in real time.
  • Sing the answer before you click it. Passive listening plateaus fast.
  • Do the chord chapters with arpeggio off. Block chords force you to hear the whole stack, not the top line.
  • Jam tab is the transfer test: loop a progression, improvise, and check yourself against the highlighted chord tones.
  • Ten focused minutes daily beats an hour once a week.
